Ellen O'Sullivan

Every day is a chance to learn.

I am a teacher of Business, Geography, Maths and Economics. I created EduEllen to bring together clear learning resources, practical tutoring and useful classroom materials — all in one place — for students and teachers who want straightforward, high-quality support.

Whether you are a student looking for revision notes and grind support, or a teacher searching for ready-to-use classroom materials, EduEllen is designed to make your work a little easier and a lot more effective.
